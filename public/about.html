Our Sri Lankan Story

Preserving Ceylon’s Rich Agricultural Legacy

Established in Colombo, Sri Lanka, Ceylon Harvest Organics (Pvt) Ltd was founded with a singular vision: to bring pure, unadulterated Sri Lankan spices and high-altitude teas directly from certified organic smallholder farms to international and local consumers.

Sri Lanka has been celebrated for over two millennia as the "Spice Island" of the Indian Ocean. Our Alba grade cinnamon from Galle, whole black pepper from Matale, and single-origin black teas from Nuwara Eliya embody centuries of traditional cultivation wisdom combined with modern sustainable farming practices.
